My Daily Routine

My day starts very early. I usually wake up at around 5:00 a.m. Then I take a shower, get dressed, drink a glass of water, and leave for college. I don't usually have breakfast because it's too early. I go to college by bus. I have to take two buses, and I usually get there at around 8:00 a.m. The trip takes about an hour and a half, but when there's heavy traffic, it can take up to two hours. To make the time go faster, I like to listen to music. I'm an intern at a small software company, and I start work at 2:00 p.m. I usually work from home, so after class I take the bus home, have lunch, and then start working. But sometimes I go to the office. On office days, I go straight from college to work. I help the team with programming tasks and stuff like that. I finish work at around 7:00 p.m. After that, I take a shower and have dinner. I live with my grandmother, and whenever she's home, there's always food ready. I really appreciate that, because after a long day I'm usually very tired and hungry. In the evening, it depends on how my day went. If it was a hard day, I like to play video games or watch a show that I like. On normal days, I take care of what I need to do, like college assignments or chores at home. I also like to spend some time with my grandmother. After that, I go to bed. On weekends, I usually wake up and have breakfast. Then, I study for a while. After that, I play video games and spend time with my family and friends. That's my routine.
